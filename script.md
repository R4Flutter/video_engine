# script.md — "₹100 a Day → ₹1 CRORE" (30s Short)

**Topic:** Compound interest — ₹100/day invested beats ₹100/day saved
**Style:** Punchy Explainer (broad appeal, fast cuts)
**Hook Strategy:** The Specificity Slam (numbers = credibility, verifiable math)
**Target Audience:** 18–25 year olds, saving-curious, finance beginners
**Goal:** Education + Follow (Conversion)

---

## 1. WHY THIS SCRIPT WORKS (the mechanism)

- The hook leads with **three concrete numbers** — no intro, no "hey guys".
- The video is **one equation**: ₹100 × 365 × 12% × 30 years = ₹1 crore.
- Every beat is a **visual proof step**: the money literally grows on screen.
- The ending **loops back to the opening coin** (retention + loopability).
- Zero hype words. The math is the drama.
