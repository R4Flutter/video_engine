# KODAK INVENTED IT. NEVER SOLD IT.

**Topic:** Kodak invented the digital camera in 1975 — then never sold it, to protect the film business that died anyway
**Style:** Vox Deep Dive (mechanism-first, cream paper)
**Format:** Portrait 9:16
**Hook Strategy:** Contradiction + Mechanism Reveal — "invented it, never sold it" is the whole story, and the refusal is what no one knows
**Target Audience:** US / tier-1, 18–30, watches business stories, has seen a Kodak but not the story
**Goal:** Story + a fact worth retelling ("They built it. 1975.")

---

## 1. WHY THIS SCRIPT WORKS (the mechanism)

- The hook is a **contradiction in six words** — "Kodak invented it. Never sold it." invents and refuses in the same breath, so the viewer has a question before the video has a second beat.
- The mechanism is the reveal: film was **90% of Kodak's money**, so every digital photo would have killed a print. The decision is stupid until the number lands — that turn is the retention.
- The escalation is **named competitors marching** (Sony. Canon. Fuji.) into a bankruptcy date everyone half-remembers: Chapter 11, 2012.
- The close **repeats the hook's own words** — "Kodak invented it. Never sold it. 1975." — so the loop is shut and the replay reads as continuous: one fact worth saving.
