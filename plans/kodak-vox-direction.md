## ENGINE RULES EVERY SECOND OBEYS

- **Canvas**: 1080x1920 portrait. Paper #F4F1EA fixed for the whole film. Plates (transparent PNGs) ride in the FOREGROUND, right-of-centre, over the paper.
- **Page turn**: at every beat boundary, 0.3s (9 frames) overlap — the outgoing page lifts and clears (translateY −2.5% height), the incoming page rises in (+3.5% → 0). The frame is never empty.
- **Camera**: global scale (usePlanCamera) — beats hold at 1.0, slams punch, mid-beats push slowly, payoff settles. IMPACT swell lands at 22.0s (payoff). No shake.
- **Headlines**: top-third band, engine-rendered type, on the page under the plate's clean zone.
- **Captions**: bottom band, above the phone safe strip; active word LIFTS (not recolours). Kinetic beats (1 and 7) draw NO captions — their words are already on the page.
- **Frame-zero card**: "KODAK INVENTED IT. NEVER SOLD IT." overlays frames 0–~1.0s while the kinetic beat assembles underneath; clears as the claim completes.
- **Plates**: slide from off-screen left, settle right-of-centre (x = 14% width, w = 56% width, top = 26% height), cubic ease-out, opacity = ease. Plate switches happen ON the shot windows below.
- **SFX map**: 0:00 chime · 0:02 whoosh · 0:04 rising whoosh · 0:08 notification pop (×3) · 0:13 deep boom · 0:17 rolling digits · 0:22 warm calm · music quiet from 22s.
