# BACKGROUNDS — PRODUCTION-GRADE ENVIRONMENT PROMPTS

Backgrounds are full-frame plates designed for compositing, not hero illustrations. Every plate must establish believable spatial depth, leave intentional negative space, avoid accidental readable text and brands, and remain visually compatible with the episode's restrained documentary color grade.
