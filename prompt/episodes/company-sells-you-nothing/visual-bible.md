# COMPANY SELLS YOU NOTHING — VISUAL BIBLE

## Editorial direction
World-class long-form business documentary. The visual language is editorial documentary realism, not a single illustration style.

The viewer should feel that every frame is either evidence, a real environment, a period artifact, a concrete business object, a designed information graphic, or a short realistic B-roll observation.

Do not turn the story into a generic VOX paper-cut animation.

## Visual grammar
- Primary palette: charcoal `#101214`, warm off-white `#F4F1EA`, muted steel blue, restrained Amazon yellow, Planet Fitness purple/yellow, Adobe red, and one warning red for legal/evidence moments.
- Backgrounds are dark and realistic whenever the frame is an environment.
- Evidence is neutral, flat-lit, slightly imperfect and documentary.
- Graphics are clean, restrained, high-contrast, with one highlighted number or relationship at a time.
- No decorative neon. No cartoon people. No fake 3D glass.
- Faces are rare and never used as generic visual filler.
- Historical imagery gets authentic period grain and period-correct architecture/clothing.
- UI screens are believable reconstructions; final readable copy is overlaid by the video engine.
- Transparent foreground assets have real alpha and clean edges.
- Every shot needs a reason to exist.

## Camera language
- Cold open: locked-off wides, uncomfortable holds, tiny human scale.
- Mechanism: macro inserts, top-down documents, controlled lateral moves.
- Reversals: hard cuts, symmetrical comparisons, match cuts.
- Evidence: overhead / straight-on, slow controlled push.
- Legal section: colder, flatter, less cinematic; let documents do the work.
- Landing: return to the exact cold-open composition for the strongest callback.

## Retention rules
- A meaningful visual state change at least every 4–8 seconds.
- Major reveal every ~45–90 seconds.
- No montage longer than 3 seconds per individual clip unless the narration is explicitly counting.
- Dollar figures get a beat of silence before appearance.
- Do not animate everything; alternate hold → change → evidence → hold → impact.
- Never repeat the same visual treatment twice in a row.
- Every section must introduce a new object, entity, mechanism, or question.

## Authenticity
Assets marked `SOURCE_CAPTURE` must come from the original public filing / release / court opinion. AI-generated recreations are never presented as original evidence.

## 16 GB RAM / CPU-only constraint
Keep source stills in the 2048 px class, B-roll at 1080p, use short clips, and do not make local heavy AI generation a hard dependency.
