# ARCHIVE / PERIOD RECREATIONS — PRODUCTION-GRADE PROMPTS

Archive assets must feel historically grounded without pretending a generated reconstruction is an original photograph. When a slot is a recreation, preserve the period cues and label it as a recreation in editorial metadata. Avoid modern equipment, modern typography, contemporary clothing and accidental current branding.
