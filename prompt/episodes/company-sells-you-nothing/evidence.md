# EVIDENCE — SOURCE-FIRST, GENERATION-SAFE PRODUCTION PROMPTS

Evidence assets are documentary objects, not decorative illustrations. Prefer the original public source whenever possible. If the original cannot be captured or licensing/access prevents direct use, create a clearly marked recreation and keep the metadata explicit. Never fabricate a source capture and present it as authentic. Preserve dates, entity names, legal posture, and source identity exactly as supplied by verified material.
