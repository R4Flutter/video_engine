All graphics: 2048×1152 PNG, renderer-added final type, one visual idea per scene.

## `g01_membership_density.png`
Beat 00:05 · P0
**Prompt:** Minimal editorial data composition comparing 20.8M members, 2,896 clubs and derived ~7,200 members/club ratio. Three progressively tighter blocks, charcoal background, warm-white number fields, restrained Planet Fitness purple/yellow accents. No decorative icons.
**Motion:** 20.8M → 2,896 → 7,200, each 0.4–0.6s.

## `g02_four_percent_math.png`
Beat 00:20 · P0
**Prompt:** Clean calculation: 20.8M × 4% ≈ 832,000. Then 832,000 ÷ 2,896 ≈ 287. Huge final 287, white on black with one yellow accent.
**Motion:** left-to-right calculation, final number holds 0.8s.
