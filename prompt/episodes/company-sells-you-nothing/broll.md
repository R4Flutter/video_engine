# B-ROLL — PRODUCTION-GRADE VIDEO GENERATION / SOURCING PROMPTS

Default delivery: 1920×1080 MP4, 24 or 30fps, 3–6 seconds unless otherwise stated. Every clip is designed around editability: realistic physics, intentional framing, no excessive camera movement, clean head/tail frames, no embedded captions and no invented readable text. When an exact real-world shot can be sourced legally, prefer real footage over synthetic footage. For generated footage, reproduce the requested visual language without copying a specific creator.
