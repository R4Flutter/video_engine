# Prompt 05 — Context Background / Environment Plate

Use behind transparent subjects when the story needs a location without expensive full-video footage.

```text
Create a premium cinematic editorial environment for [PLACE / CONTEXT], suitable as a reusable background plate for a business documentary.

Composition: wide establishing frame, visually calm center region for a foreground subject, important architecture/details pushed toward the outer thirds. No dominant object should compete with a later foreground cutout.

Camera: [EYE LEVEL / LOW / HIGH], realistic 35mm documentary perspective.
Lighting: believable natural or motivated practical lighting.
Mood: [TENSION / AMBITION / CURIOSITY / CALM / COLLAPSE].
Color: restrained, sophisticated, slightly desaturated, cream/charcoal/deep-green/gold family where appropriate.

The environment must feel physically real and spatially coherent. Avoid surreal architecture, floating objects, fake text, random crowds, excessive bokeh, neon, lens flare or decorative particles.
No people in the foreground unless explicitly requested; leave the hero zone clean.
```
