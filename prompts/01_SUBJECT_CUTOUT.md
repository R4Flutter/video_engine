# Prompt 01 — Transparent Hero Subject Cutout

Use for the exact object/person/product that the editor will animate independently.

```text
[SUBJECT]
Create a premium editorial cutout of [SUBJECT], isolated completely from the background.

View: [PERFECT SIDE PROFILE / 3-QUARTER / FRONT], facing [LEFT / RIGHT].
Pose / state: [POSE OR ACTION].
Framing: subject centered with generous transparent padding, occupying about 65–75% of the canvas.
Visual job: the subject must read instantly at 1080×1920 and remain recognizable when scaled down.
Geometry: clean silhouette, accurate proportions, crisp edges, realistic contact geometry, no accidental cropping.
Lighting: soft controlled studio lighting with believable edge separation; no dramatic colored rim lights.
Material detail: preserve realistic surface detail but avoid excessive microtexture that becomes noise in video.

TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND: mandatory, true alpha channel.
No floor, no shadow plate, no reflection, no environment, no text, no logo unless the logo is part of the real subject.
Create a single reusable hero asset, not a poster.
```

Append `00_BASE_STYLE_LOCK.md`.

Recommended variants: `hero-left`, `hero-right`, `detail`, `front`.
