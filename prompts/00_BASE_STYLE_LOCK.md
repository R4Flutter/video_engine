# BASE STYLE LOCK — Business / Finance Documentary Assets

Append this block to every generation prompt in this folder.

```text
Premium editorial business-documentary visual language, designed for a professional YouTube finance/business channel. Cinematic but restrained. Strong photographic realism for archival or subject imagery; precise information design for graphics. Clear subject hierarchy, deliberate negative space, sophisticated composition, natural perspective, believable lighting, subtle filmic contrast, no plastic CGI look, no generic stock-photo smile, no visual clutter.

Palette: warm cream paper #F4F1EA, charcoal #1A1A1A, restrained deep green #16A34A or muted gold #C89B3C as a single accent depending on scene. Avoid gradients unless physically present in the photographed source. Avoid neon colors.

Composition must be usable inside a 9:16 vertical YouTube Short and a 16:9 long-form crop. Keep the main subject inside a central 70% safe region and leave intentional negative space for typography. Never bake text, captions, UI, watermarks, logos or decorative borders into the image unless the prompt explicitly asks for a document/artifact.

High-end editorial art direction, modern business journalism, premium documentary frame, visually expensive through composition and evidence rather than excessive effects. No lens flare, no glowing outlines, no floating particles, no fake depth-of-field blur, no exaggerated HDR, no random icons.
```
