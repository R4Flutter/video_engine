# Prompt 02 — Archival / Documentary Photo

Use for historical people, companies, factories, offices, products and events.

```text
Create a documentary-grade archival photograph of [SUBJECT / EVENT / PLACE] in [YEAR / ERA].

Historical authenticity is the priority: clothing, vehicles, architecture, signage, camera technology and materials must be period-appropriate. Composition should feel like a real editorial photograph selected by a serious business journalist, not a modern recreation posing as history.

Shot type: [WIDE ESTABLISHING / MEDIUM / CLOSE DETAIL].
Camera position: [ANGLE].
Subject placement: [LEFT / RIGHT / CENTER], leaving deliberate negative space for headline typography.
Action / moment: [SPECIFIC MOMENT THAT EXPLAINS THE SENTENCE].
Lighting: believable period/location lighting, slightly imperfect exposure, subtle documentary contrast.
Texture: authentic photographic grain only; no cinematic orange-teal grade.

Do not add modern logos, modern cars, modern phones, contemporary clothing or invented readable statistics. No watermarks. No text overlays. No AI-looking faces.
```

Append `00_BASE_STYLE_LOCK.md`.

Recommended variants: wide context, medium subject, close evidence detail.
