# Prompt 06 — Brand / Logo Asset

Use for a company brand mark or product mark that will be composited independently.

```text
Create a clean, faithful isolated brand mark for [BRAND].

The logo must match the official visual identity: correct proportions, geometry, letterforms and color relationships. No redesign, no 3D extrusion, no glossy treatment, no bevel, no glow.

Output: transparent background, centered with generous padding, crisp edges, no mockup, no wall, no business card, no text other than the actual logo artwork.

Create a production asset suitable for scaling, cropping and compositing in a documentary edit. If the exact official logo cannot be faithfully reproduced, generate a neutral placeholder shape rather than inventing brand details.
```

Append `00_BASE_STYLE_LOCK.md`.
