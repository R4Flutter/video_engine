# Prompt 03 — Document / Evidence Asset

Use for filings, reports, invoices, receipts, statements, newspaper clippings, product specs and other factual artifacts.

```text
Create a believable editorial evidence artifact for [DOCUMENT TYPE] connected to [SUBJECT / COMPANY / EVENT].

The artifact should look like a real primary-source document photographed or scanned for a serious business documentary. Use realistic paper, margins, typography hierarchy, tables, stamps, lines and document structure, but DO NOT invent factual numbers, names, dates, signatures or legal claims.

Known facts to display exactly:
[PASTE VERIFIED FACTS ONLY]

Composition: slight 3/4 overhead view or near-flat scan, document fills approximately 70–85% of the frame, with one key region clearly emphasized by a restrained marker/box treatment that the editor can reproduce separately.
Lighting: neutral desk/document light, subtle paper shadow, realistic edge curl if appropriate.
Color: off-white paper, charcoal ink, one muted accent only.

No fake company seals, no fake government insignia, no invented handwriting, no decorative UI, no watermark, no dramatic glow.
```

Append `00_BASE_STYLE_LOCK.md`.

For generated visuals, the renderer treats the artifact as supporting evidence; it does not pretend generated text is independently verified.
