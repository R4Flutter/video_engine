# Fresh Channel — Ordered Checklist

Why: the old channel (`@PureSatisfy-b2c`) went from ~1,000 views per Short in June to
3–7 views in July, coinciding with reuploads of another creator's videos, and has had
zero Shorts-feed distribution since. Your work is 100% original now, so there is nothing
worth inheriting from that history.

Everything you built carries over. This is about 30 minutes.

---

## Order matters — do 1 before 2

### 1. Clear the old channel first

**This must happen before you upload anything to the new channel.**

- [ ] **Delete** the @jacksdiningroom reuploads (Content → Shorts → select → Delete forever).
      Not Private — delete. They're a live copyright strike risk regardless of the rest.
- [ ] **Delete** the finance Short "Your Savings Account Pays 0.4%."
      If it stays public on the old channel and you upload it to the new one, the new
      channel's first video is a duplicate of an existing public video. That is the exact
      signal you're trying to escape.
- [ ] Set every remaining Short to Private, or delete the channel entirely. Either is fine.

### 2. Create the new channel

Same Google account is fine — the old channel has no strikes (Notices column: "—"), and
suppression attaches to a channel, not to your Google account.

- [ ] youtube.com → your avatar → **Switch account → Create a channel**
- [ ] Name: `Real Return`
- [ ] Handle: `@realreturn`, or the first available from the list in `CHANNEL_SETUP.md` §2

### 3. Identity — paste from `CHANNEL_SETUP.md`

- [ ] Description → §3

- [ ] Picture → `avatar_b_800x800.png` (variant **B**)

### 5. First upload

- [ ] Schedule for **8:30–11:30 PM IST**

---

## What to watch now

Forget views for the first two weeks. One number tells you whether the fresh start worked:

**Analytics → Reach → "How viewers find this Short."**

If **Shorts feed** appears at all as a traffic source — even at 20 views — distribution is
working and you're in a normal cold start. That's the win condition.

If after 7 daily uploads Shorts feed is still absent, something else is going on and it's
worth stopping to diagnose rather than pushing through.

---

## The rule that caused all this

Never upload content you didn't make. Not clips, not reposts, not "edited" reuploads.
Beyond the distribution damage, it permanently blocks monetization and invites strikes.

Your Remotion pipeline produces original work end to end. Keep it that way and this
problem cannot recur.
